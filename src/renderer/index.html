TM Phone

iPhone & iPad diagnostics for the bench

Plug in an iPhone or iPad (unlocked, with “Trust This Computer” tapped) to read its full identity, battery health, storage, software and ownership status. Read-only — nothing is changed on the device.

⚠️ Erase & flash this device

This permanently wipes everything on the device and installs a fresh, Apple-signed iOS. There is no undo. Make a backup first if the data matters.

Checking Find My / Activation Lock…

Before you continue: Find My must be OFF (Settings → [name] → Find My → iPhone). If it's on, the device will be Activation-Locked after the wipe and demand the owner's Apple ID — an unusable brick.

First time with a given model/iOS? Test on a device that isn't a customer's. Don't disconnect during the flash.

Checking for Apple device tools…

Setup needed — open the Downloads tab

  1. Go to the Downloads tab and install the Apple Mobile Device driver and the libimobiledevice tools (one click each).
  2. Connect a device, unlock it and tap Trust, then press Read device.
